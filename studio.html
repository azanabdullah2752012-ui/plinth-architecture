THE STUDIO

A considered approach to place.

PLINTH is an independent architecture and spatial design studio based in Lisbon, Portugal. Our work spans architecture, interiors, heritage renovation and temporary installations, guided by an enduring interest in place, material and light.

Interior study: natural light and tectonic honesty in domestic space.

PHILOSOPHY

Three guiding commitments.

Place

Every project begins with its context. We study the character, history and conditions of a site before proposing a response.

Material

Materials should express what they are. Concrete, stone, timber and plaster are allowed to retain their natural character rather than imitate something else.

Time

Good architecture is not designed to chase a trend. We favour patient decisions, enduring proportions and spaces that remain meaningful over time.

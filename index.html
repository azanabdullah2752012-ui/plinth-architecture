Plate 01

Biblioteca Central

Porto, Portugal

An investigation into acoustic silence and overhead illumination. The coffered concrete roof structure filters indirect northern sunlight through deep reveal channels, bathing the reading hall in a soft, non-glare luminosity throughout the Atlantic seasons.

Typology: Civic Library Gross Area: 3,850 m² Structure: In-situ concrete & bog oak Status: Completed 2024
Plate 02

Edifício Vento

Cascais, Portugal · 2023

A coastal workplace anchored around a double-height ventilation court. High-performance micro-cement and sandblasted pine create quiet thermal comfort facing the Atlantic wind.

Plate 03

Pátio das Flores

Évora, Portugal · 2024

Consolidation of an eighteenth-century domestic courtyard. Hand-shaped local terracotta tiles and breathable slaked-lime wash retain moisture while respecting centuries of archaeological layering.

Plate 04

Pavilhão
Sereno

Alentejo, Portugal · 2023

A minimal horizontal roof plane suspended above dry wheat grasslands. Four slender blackened steel columns dissolve into the distant cork oak horizon, providing shade with near-zero ground footprint.

Twelve practitioners.
One discipline.

Founded in Lisbon in 2018, PLINTH operates as an integrated spatial studio encompassing architects, structural researchers, and conservation artisans. We maintain a deliberate ceiling of six active commissions per year to ensure partner immersion in every detail.

Our working method draws equally from the Portuguese tectonic lineage of Álvaro Siza and Fernando Távora, alongside rigorous contemporary environmental modeling. We do not apply pre-determined aesthetics; the geology and culture of the site dictate the outcome.

03 // THE GLASHÜTTE BENCH

One Watchmaker, One Movement

From rough turning to final casing, each timepiece is entrusted to a single master watchmaker through two complete assembly and regulation cycles.

PHASE 01

First Assembly & Pre-Timing

The movement is dry-assembled to check gear teeth end-shake, wheel depthing, and pallet stone engagement under 40x magnification.

PHASE 02

Hand Finishing & Anglage

Every bridge edge is filed with boxwood laps to a 45-degree mirror chamfer. Screw heads are flat-polished on zinc blocks with diamond rouge.

PHASE 03

Second Assembly & Regulation

The finished components are lubricated with synthetic Moebius oils and timed in six static positions across three temperature gradients.

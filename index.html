In-Situ Lime Plaster & Travertine

Mouraria Old Quarter, Lisbon · Local Quarry Block 4

Hand-troweled lime wash over hydrated calcium carbonate stone. Breathes with Atlantic coastal humidity and reflects shifting afternoon light without synthetic gloss.

Compressive Strength 18.4 MPa

Twelve practitioners.
One discipline.

Founded in Lisbon in 2018, PLINTH operates as an integrated spatial studio encompassing architects, structural researchers, and conservation artisans. We maintain a deliberate ceiling of six active commissions per year to ensure partner immersion in every detail.

Our working method draws directly from the Portuguese tectonic lineage of Álvaro Siza and Fernando Távora, alongside rigorous contemporary environmental modeling. We do not apply pre-determined aesthetics; the geology and culture of the site dictate the outcome.
